Bard Spell Performance Advice

Bard Spell Performance Creation

This is the process of creating poetry, songs, dances, paintings, sketches or other artistic expressions as part of casting your bard spells in a TTRPG. How you do this is based on rules about spell casting and Bards (or their equivalent) in the particular game and table you are playing at.

Rules About Spell Casting and Bards

Before you create bard spell performances you need to consider the limitations placed upon these performances based on the game you are playing and the table rules variations. A good rule of thumb is to discuss it with your Game Master prior to playing a Bard. An example of one rule that can limit these performances are the rules on spell casting in Dungeons & Dragons 5.5e, which state that the words used to cast a spell cannot be actual words in a language Casting Spells in D&D. Now this creates potentially problematic limitations depending on how you want to perform spells. However, the bard spell casting lore means that some Game Masters will not be so strict with this ruling. Nonetheless, even with this restriction you can still do quite a lot including:

  • Draw random symbols that meaning nothing in any language
  • Scatting like they do in Jazz songs
  • Chants in Gregorian chant style with meaningless gobbledygook
  • Dance
  • Paint

Safety Considerations

This section I choose to write separately to the rules section because not everyone will consider this issue in what the rules are around spell performance. I address this because I had to consider what was Safe for me to do with other people at a table I was playing at. I had taken the approach of performing spells the tune of parts of well known songs that I changed the words to. I was given permission to use this approach so I could use actual words in songs. One of the song I considered for a healing spell was Sexual Healing by Marvin Gaye with the words changed to ‘When you get that feeling I’m giving you magical healing, magical healing, magical healing’. Now given the words are originally sexual healing I decided not to use this song as it was inappropriate and could have been unsafe for other players. I could have asked if I could use it, but I thought it best to pick a different option despite thinking that it would be hilarious if I could use that song with the right group.

Creating Bard Spell Performances

Now that you know what the limitations are based on rules and safety considerations it’s time to speak about the creation process. We need a performance that can be completed within an appropriate timeframe so your turns do not take forever. Before we make the next step we need to set aside time before you start playing the character to create and practice the performance so they are ready to go or you just use them as you develop them. The secret to this is start by writing down at least 3 ideas that would work with the rules and safety considerations. Then consider how you can reduce the time it takes to perform a spell using these different ideas whilst still being true to the ideas and still enjoying the performance. You can use a combination of performance styles or just one. I would suggest the first time you do this just pick one. Now you can start creating the performances by developing some drafts of how it would apply to different songs in your characters repertoire of spells. I would start with a spell you think you will use often or one you are really excited about performing. Then you just refine them until you are happy with them.

Practice Your Spell Performances

Once you have created the final version of a spell performance you then need to practice it prior to using it. I would recommend 2 weeks of daily practice to begin with and then regular practice several times each week while you are playing the character to maintain your memory. I would even do this if you are planning to improvise your performance as practising creativity helps to maintain it. Some of this practice in front of others will help you build confidence and helps you to learn to manage your nerves and still complete a satisfactory performance.

Performing Your Spells

Performing your spells will be very intimidating for some people. However, if you have practiced in front of others before and you have a supportive table you should be able to handle it. The more you do it the more confident and comfortable you will become. It will also bring more enjoyment to your table and encourage other player at your table to engage in roleplay because they will see how much you enjoy it and the positive response it gets from the table (provided you have a supportive table).
